❓ Frequently asked

What does reliability (r) mean?

Correlation between pre‑ and post‑measurement (or true‑score proportion). r=0 means pure luck, r=1 means no regression.

How is predicted true score calculated?

Kelley’s formula: r·X + (1‑r)·μ. It shrinks the observed score toward the mean.

Why does regression happen?

Extreme scores contain more measurement error; upon retest, scores tend to move toward the population mean.
