📖 FAQ

What is Cohen's d? Standardized difference: (M1−M2)/pooled SD. Small=0.2, Medium=0.5, Large=0.8.

How is Hedges' g different? It applies a bias correction to Cohen's d, better for small samples.

What does CLES mean? Probability that a random person from Group 1 exceeds Group 2. 0.5 = no effect.
