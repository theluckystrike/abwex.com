# A/B Test Calculator — Frequentist & Bayesian Statistical Significance Calculator

**[Analyze Your Test →](https://abwex.com)** | [About](https://abwex.com/about.html) | [Blog](https://abwex.com/blog/)

A/B Test Calculator helps you determine whether your experiment results are statistically significant. Supports both Frequentist (z-test, chi-squared) and Bayesian analysis methods. Handle multi-variant tests (A/B/C/D), estimate revenue impact, and calculate required sample sizes before you run. Built for product managers, growth engineers, and data analysts who need fast, reliable experiment analysis.

## Features

- **Frequentist and Bayesian analysis** — choose the statistical framework that fits your team
- **Multi-variant support** — compare up to 4 variations simultaneously (A/B/C/D)
- **Revenue impact estimation** — translate conversion lifts into projected dollar impact
- **Sample size calculator** — determine how long to run your test before starting
- **Confidence interval visualization** — see the range of plausible effects graphically

## How It Works

Enter your control and variant data — visitors and conversions for each group. Select Frequentist or Bayesian mode. The Frequentist engine computes the z-score, p-value, and confidence intervals. The Bayesian engine calculates the probability that each variant beats the control using Beta distributions. For multi-variant tests, the calculator applies proper multiple comparison corrections. Revenue impact projections convert your lift percentage into annual dollar estimates based on your inputs.
