About Abwex

Systematic experimentation methodology for teams that ship better models.

Our Mission

Abwex was founded on the observation that the difference between high-performing ML teams and struggling ones is rarely talent or compute — it is methodology. Teams that run rigorous experiments, track every result, and make decisions based on statistical evidence consistently outperform teams that rely on intuition and ad-hoc workflows. We teach the discipline of systematic experimentation because it is the highest-leverage skill improvement available to most ML teams.

Our content covers the full experimentation lifecycle: designing controlled experiments that isolate the effect of each change, setting up experiment tracking systems that make every run reproducible and comparable, running hyperparameter optimization that efficiently explores large search spaces, conducting A/B tests in production with proper statistical rigor, and building rapid prototyping workflows that compress the time from idea to validated result.

We draw on established practices from experimental science, clinical trials, and industrial statistics, adapting them to the specific challenges of machine learning. The principles are not new — the scientific method has been refined for centuries — but their application to ML experimentation requires domain-specific knowledge about data leakage, overfitting to evaluation metrics, and the unique reproducibility challenges of stochastic training processes.

Abwex is part of a network of specialized AI and ML resources. Our flagship resource, zovo.one, provides a comprehensive directory of AI tools spanning the entire ecosystem. Together with our partner sites, we cover AI development from foundational research to production deployment.
